What it does

Six tools, all pointed at the material you already have.

Most study apps hand you someone else's content. Mentra works from yours — the lecture slides, the past questions, the textbook chapter you photographed.

A tutor that read your file

Ask a question and get an answer grounded in your document, with the page it came from. If your material doesn't cover it, Mentra says so instead of inventing an answer.

Summaries that fit the subject

A plain-language overview, the points worth revising, and an exam note — all drawn from that specific file, never a generic example borrowed from another subject.

Flashcards and 30-question quizzes

Generated from your material at your class level, with an explanation for every answer you get wrong.

A solver that shows its working

Type a maths or science problem, or photograph it. You get the method step by step, the final answer with units, and a check on whether it makes sense.

A planner that actually reminds you

Put study sessions, revision and exams on a calendar. Your phone alerts you at the time you chose, whether or not you have data.

Reading progress worth trusting

Mentra remembers how far into each file you got and reopens it there — no hunting for where you stopped last night.

How it works

Three steps, then it's yours.

STEP 1

Add your material

PDFs, Word documents, slides, spreadsheets, notes, or a photo of a page. Up to 25 MB each. Everything stays private to your account.

STEP 2

Ask, or let it summarise

Put your question in your own words. Mentra finds the relevant pages and answers from them, citing where it looked.

STEP 3

Turn it into practice

Build flashcards or a quiz from the same file, then schedule the revision so you actually come back to it.

Who it's for

Primary school through university.

Mentra asks your country, level and class when you sign up, then pitches explanations to match. A JSS 2 student and a 300-level undergraduate get different answers to the same question.
